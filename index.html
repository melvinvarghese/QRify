A few quick answers

Good to know

Does QRify send my content anywhere?

No. The generator and QR library run in this browser. QRify does not add analytics or store your content.

Can QRify tell me whether a link is safe?

No. QRify encodes the information you enter; it does not scan links or verify where they lead. Check a link before sharing it.

What can someone see when they scan my code?

They can read the information encoded in it. Avoid putting anything in a QR code that you do not want a recipient to see.
